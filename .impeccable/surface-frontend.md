# Surface: CineTracker App UI

Mode: Operate
Target: frontend/src
Seed key: ccb07493

## Direction contract

THESIS: A dense, ruled grid of boxed modules — every title a cell, every stat a column — where the catalogue reads as a working reference document before it reads as a designed object. Refuses the poster-hero scroll and the streaming-app dark-on-dark default.

OWN-WORLD: Bright-white module borders (1px hairline) on a near-black ground (#0d0d12). Primary accent #00BFFF for tabs, active states, and one signature move. Utility label typography in a compact gothic sans (system-ui at 11–13px, weight 500–700). Rating and status use distinct role-colors: completed #4fcf70, watching #00BFFF, pending #f7c04f, dropped #f74f4f. Data numbers in tabular-figures monospace. No gradients on text. No colored border-left/right above 1px on cards.

STORY: The user opens the app, reads their catalogue at a glance as a dense ordered grid of module cells, taps a cell to log a watch or change status, checks their stats in a clean data panel, and closes. Sessions are 1–5 minutes.

FIRST VIEWPORT (Home): Left sidebar 224px — logo + nav items + user block. Main area: a two-row summary strip (5 stat pills in hairline-bordered cells) at the top, then a section "Recommended for You" with a 6-column poster grid of boxed TitleCard modules, then "Recently Added" same grid. No hero, no splash image.

FORM: Japanese high-density web grid elevated with condensed stats typography and split-flap 90ms step-transition as signature move on status change. Assigned candidate #3 from seed ccb07493. Challengers 4 (wins), 1 (rescated cascade move), 5 (rescated labeled segments).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
